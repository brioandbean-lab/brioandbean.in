Our Story

Brio & Bean was created to explore India's rich coffee-growing regions and share their stories through exceptional coffee. Every origin is a stop on the trail, every cup a new discovery. Our journey begins in Karnataka and will continue across India's most celebrated coffee landscapes.

Trail 01 — House Blend

The first stop on The Coffee Trail. A balanced specialty coffee crafted for everyday brewing, featuring rich chocolate notes, gentle sweetness and a smooth finish. Available as whole beans or freshly ground coffee. Origin: Karnataka, India.
